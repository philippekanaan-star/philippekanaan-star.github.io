Frequently asked questions

What do you mean by a "tracked account"?

A customer in your portfolio that marsā scores and monitors.

I have few users but a lot of accounts?

Then it's your volume that determines the number of licenses. A portfolio of 1,200 tracked accounts managed by two people needs three licenses, or €1,470 per month — and you get all three seats, even if only two are in use today.

How long before I see something?

Days, not months. marsā plugs into standard connectors and requires no instrumentation of your product. We backfill your history at setup so the score is useful from day one.

Where is my data stored?

In Europe, with European hosting providers. Content generated by artificial intelligence is labeled as such, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act. The processing register and data processing agreement are provided with the product.

Can I change my plan later?

At any time, up or down, effective the following month. Each user has their own individual seat — which is what lets the decision log attribute every action to the right person.
