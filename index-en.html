The problem

Churn doesn't strike suddenly. It sets in, slowly, under the radar.

By the time notice arrives, the decision was made months ago. In the meantime, the signals were there — scattered across tools nobody has time to cross-reference.

Annoyed ticket Logins ↓ Champion left Invoice overdue
Isolated, invisible. Together, a signal.

The signals are scattered

An annoyed support ticket, a drop in logins, a champion changing roles, an overdue invoice. None of these signals is alarming on its own. Together, they tell the story of a customer leaving.

CSMs fly blind

Two hundred accounts in a portfolio, eight hours in a day. Time spent digging through tools is time stolen from the customer relationship — and it mostly produces unverifiable hunches.

Prioritization runs on instinct

The loudest account jumps the queue ahead of the quiet one. But the customer leaving quietly doesn't complain: they simply stop logging in, then stop renewing.

With loya, you cut your churn by up to 30% and build lasting customer loyalty.

Our stance

Four choices we hold ourselves to.

The score is deterministic. AI just drafts.

The calculation rests on readable rules and weightings, not an opaque model. AI only steps in to summarize and phrase things. The result: a score you can defend in front of your leadership and an auditor alike.

Human validation by default.

No action is ever sent to a customer without a CSM reviewing it first. Automation will come later, on the low-touch segment, and only after a measured observation period.

European by design.

Data hosted in the EU, strict isolation between clients, pseudonymization before any model call, and zero training on your data. It's not a toggle you can switch on — it's the architecture.

Live in days, not quarters.

Established platforms charge several thousand euros in setup fees and roll out over a quarter. loya starts from a CSV import and a first read of your portfolio, then connectors get plugged in.

Compliance & security

Frequently asked questions

What we get asked the most.

How is this different from another dashboard? +

A dashboard shows you a state and leaves you to draw the conclusions. loya goes all the way to the recommendation: which account to prioritize, why, and what to write to them. loya's output isn't a chart, it's a prepared decision the CSM approves or rejects.

How do you avoid false positives? +

The score is calibrated on your past churn, not on generic thresholds. Every recommendation a CSM accepts, edits or rejects — and their reason — feeds that calibration. It's a system that improves with use, not a fixed model.

Does loya write directly to my customers? +

No. No action is ever sent to a customer without a CSM reviewing and approving it. loya prepares the draft and makes the case for the recommendation; the decision to send always stays human.

Is my data used to train a model? +

No. Zero model training on your data — it isn't a setting you can toggle off, it's the architecture. Your data is pseudonymized before any call to a model.

How long until we're up and running? +

loya starts from a CSV import and a first read of your portfolio within days, then OAuth connectors are plugged in progressively — without the months-long integration project established platforms require.

Why don't you publish your pricing? +

Because the first question isn't the price, it's eligibility: below a certain volume of accounts and history, no solution on the market — ours included — can demonstrate its value. Pricing is shared after a short qualification call.
